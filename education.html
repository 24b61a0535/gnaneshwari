🔍 How to Recycle Right

  • Plastic: Wash out food containers before recycling.
  • Paper: Keep it dry! Greasy pizza boxes go in the bin, not recycling.
  • Glass: Remove metal caps and lids.

💡 Did You Know?

Recycling a single aluminium can saves enough energy to run a laptop for 3 hours!
